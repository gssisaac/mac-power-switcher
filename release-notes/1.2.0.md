## Mac Power Switcher 1.2.0

Menu bar app for macOS 13+ that keeps your Mac awake, then sleeps it after the lid has been closed for a time you choose.

### Changes

- Wake mode stays on while the lid is open. The Mac does not count down during use, and the menu bar no longer shows a timer.
- The time starts when the lid closes. A 30-minute setting sleeps the Mac 30 minutes after the lid closes. 10 minutes sleeps it 10 minutes after the lid closes.
- Opening the lid cancels that time and keeps the Mac awake. Closing the lid again starts the full time over.
- Wake mode stays on after a timed sleep, so you do not have to turn it on again.

### Install

1. Download `MacPowerSwitcher.dmg` from this release.
2. Open the DMG and drag **Mac Power Switcher** to **Applications**.
3. Launch from Applications; the app lives in the menu bar only (no Dock icon).
4. If an older copy is already running, quit it from the menu bar, then open the new one.

### Notes

- The first time you turn wake mode on, macOS may ask for an administrator password to install a small helper under `/Library/MacPowerSwitcher`.
- Low-battery auto-off still does not ask for Touch ID.
