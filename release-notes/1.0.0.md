## Mac Power Switcher 1.0.0

Menu bar app for macOS 13+ that toggles **sleep prevention when the lid is closed** (via `pmset`).

### Install

1. Download `MacPowerSwitcher.dmg` from this release.
2. Open the DMG and drag **Mac Power Switcher** to **Applications**.
3. Launch from Applications; the app lives in the menu bar only (no Dock icon).

### Notes

- The first time you enable sleep prevention, macOS may ask for Touch ID or your password, and once for an administrator password to install a small helper under `/Library/MacPowerSwitcher`.
- Optional settings include a low-battery auto-off threshold and biometric confirmation when changing the setting manually.
