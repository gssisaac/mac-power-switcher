## Mac Power Switcher 1.1.0

Menu bar app for macOS 13+ that keeps your Mac awake when the lid is closed (via `pmset`).

### Changes

- The menu is now **Turn on wake up**, **Turn off wake up**, and **Settings**.
- Touch ID or your password is asked once after launch, not on every toggle.
- Wake mode stays on when you open the lid. Turn on **Turn off when the lid opens** in Settings if you want the old behavior.
- Closing the lid starts the wake timeout over. A 15-minute timer with 2 minutes left becomes 15 minutes again. This can be turned off in Settings.
- While the lid is open, the time left appears beside the menu bar icon as `hh:mm` (for example `00:14` or `01:05`).

### Install

1. Download `MacPowerSwitcher.dmg` from this release.
2. Open the DMG and drag **Mac Power Switcher** to **Applications**.
3. Launch from Applications; the app lives in the menu bar only (no Dock icon).

### Notes

- The first time you turn wake mode on, macOS may ask for an administrator password to install a small helper under `/Library/MacPowerSwitcher`.
- Low-battery auto-off still does not ask for Touch ID.
